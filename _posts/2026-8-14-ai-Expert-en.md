**AGENT Expert Understanding**

**Architecture Design**
- [ ] Can design an overall architecture consisting of multiple agents and multiple MCP servers
- [ ] Can create and optimize CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md and design custom Subagents (description/tools/model settings)
- [ ] Can extract repetitive work as Skills and embed enforced rules with Hooks

**Parallelism / Automation**
- [ ] Can decompose tasks into independent subtasks and instruct parallel execution via fan-out (including worktree separation)
- [ ] Can design and monitor automated tasks with scheduled/API/GitHub-event triggers
- [ ] Understands the "context engineering" to keep autonomy and data consistency in a parallel system

**Measurement / Quality**
- [ ] Does not tolerate flaky tests and can design deterministic tests that act as gates for autonomous workflows
- [ ] Can design quantitative metrics (PR acceptance rate, coverage) to measure the effectiveness of AI adoption
- [ ] Can judge the tradeoff between parallelism and cost, and prevent runaway automation (forgetting to stop)
