**AGENT Steward Understanding**

**Strategy / Investment Decisions**
- [ ] Can formulate an organization-wide AI/agent adoption roadmap
- [ ] Can objectively diagnose the organization's ACMM level and start from the mechanisms needed for the next transition without skipping levels
- [ ] Understands that technical debt directly degrades AI output quality and can prioritize refactoring as investment
- [ ] Can design a codebase that serves as the AI's operating manual (tests = trust boundary, instructions = decision criteria, metrics = priority)

**Governance / Organization**
- [ ] Can oversee and standardize organization-wide CLAUDE.md/Skills/Hooks/guidelines
- [ ] Can design role separation and information handoff across multiple agents, localizing the impact of failures
- [ ] Can measure ROI/productivity and onboard other members
- [ ] Can design operations that run without humans (community/organization-driven Issue-to-implementation pipelines)
